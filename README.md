# AI Fraud & Agentic Risk Decision Engine

> An AI-powered fraud risk decision platform that combines machine learning, rule-based detection, explainability, agentic investigation, risk policy, audit logging, and real-time monitoring into a unified fintech-style dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Overview

The **AI Fraud & Agentic Risk Decision Engine** is an end-to-end fraud detection and risk decisioning platform.

It takes a financial transaction as input and passes it through multiple layers of intelligence:

```text
Transaction
    ↓
XGBoost Fraud Model
    ↓
Risk Engine
    ↓
Fraud Rules
    ↓
SHAP Explainability
    ↓
AI Risk Agent
    ↓
Risk Policy
    ↓
APPROVE / REVIEW / BLOCK
    ↓
Audit + Monitoring
    ↓
React Dashboard
